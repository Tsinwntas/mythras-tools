Special Effects depend on relative success levels, even when no damage is inflicted. A defender may gain the higher success level and still suffer injury.

1. Compare success levels

Compare the Level of Success. The winner chooses their Special Effects.

  • Offensive effects
  • Defensive effects
  • Spirit combat effects
  • Natural weapon effects · p. 222

If an effect requires an Opposed Test, make it now and compare it with the original Attack or Parry value.

2. Resolve damage

  1. If Choose Location was not selected, roll the hit location.
  2. If the attacker succeeded, roll damage and apply any Damage Bonus.
  3. If Passive Blocking covers that location, reduce the damage.
  4. A successful defense can reduce damage further.
  5. Apply armour and protective magic to the remaining damage.
